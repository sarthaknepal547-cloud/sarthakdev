About Me

Discover who I am and what drives me

Hello, I'm Sarthak Dev Nepal

Born and raised in the beautiful city of Bhaktapur, I'm a 17-year-old with a curious mind and a heart full of dreams. As a member of the Suddha Bahun community, I've grown up surrounded by rich cultural traditions that have shaped my perspective on life.

From a young age, I've been fascinated by the world around me - from the ancient architecture of my hometown to the digital landscapes of the internet. I believe in living life to the fullest, embracing new experiences, and constantly learning and growing.

My journey is just beginning, and I'm excited to share it with you through this website. Here, you'll find glimpses into my daily life, my passions, and the things that make me who I am.
